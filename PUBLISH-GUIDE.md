# 📖 PUBLISH GUIDE — Put your website live (free)

Your whole website is in the folder: `C:\Users\amin\maakehatonkaacchar`

## Option 1: Netlify Drop (easiest — 2 minutes, free)

1. Open the website: **app.netlify.com/drop** in your browser
2. Drag your whole folder into the page (yes, the whole folder, not just one file)
3. Wait a few seconds — done!
4. Your website is now live at a free address like: `https://your-project-name.netlify.app`
   - This is the link you share with customers

> To update the website later (new price, new photo):
> 1. Edit the files (see EDIT GUIDE)
> 2. Go back to app.netlify.com/drop and drag the folder again — the link stays the same

## Option 2: GitHub Pages (also free, for later)

## Make it an app on your phone (no Play Store needed)

Your website is already an "installable app" (PWA):

- **Android**: open your website link in Chrome → tap the ⋮ menu → **"Add to Home screen"** / **"Install app"** → it installs like a real app with our pickle icon
- **iPhone**: open in Safari → tap the Share button → **"Add to Home Screen"**

Customers don't need to download anything — they can also just open the link. Both work.
