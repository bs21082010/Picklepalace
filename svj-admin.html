How to update your site:
1. Tap Edit Website — the editor opens.
2. Change anything: products, prices, WhatsApp number, address.
3. Tap Save & Download — 3 files download.
4. Save the files to your computer's Downloads folder.
5. On your computer, double-click deploy-update.ps1 — the website and app update in ~1 minute.
Tip: the editor remembers nothing itself — the files you download ARE the new website data.
